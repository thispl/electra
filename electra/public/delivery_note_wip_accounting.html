1. Purpose

This document explains, in simple terms, how accounting (ledger) entries are created when goods are delivered to a customer using a Delivery Note in the Electra ERP system.

It covers the two delivery documents used in the system:

  • Delivery Note — the main posting document used to issue goods to a customer (Sales flow).
  • Delivery Note WIP — a working / tracking document used to record deliveries that are still in progress (Project flow).

The document also covers the Material Transfer Stock Entry that is auto-created when a DN WIP is submitted, and the Material Issue Stock Entry that is created from the Stock In Hand WIP report after invoicing — including their corresponding accounting entries.

2. Posting vs Non-posting Documents

Type Meaning Creates Accounting Entries?
Posting document A document that affects the books of accounts (General Ledger) and stock registers. Yes
Non-posting document A document used only to record or track activity. It does not affect the books. No

In the Electra system:

  • Delivery Note is a posting document — it creates Stock Ledger Entries and GL entries directly.
  • Delivery Note WIP is a non-posting document — it does not create SLE or GL entries directly, but it auto-creates a Material Transfer Stock Entry on submission which does.
Key point: The DN WIP itself does not post to the ledger. However, submitting a DN WIP triggers the system to automatically create and submit a Material Transfer Stock Entry, which does create stock and accounting entries.

3. Two Business Flows in Electra ERP

Electra ERP handles two distinct types of sales transactions, each with a different document flow and different timing for stock movement and accounting entries:

Sales Flow
Sales Order → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice
  ↑ stock out + COGS
         ↑ revenue booked
Stock and COGS booked at delivery time. Used for regular trading sales.
Project Flow
Sales Order → DN WIP → Sales Invoice → Material Issue
  ↑ auto Material Transfer (stock → WIP)
       ↑ revenue + COGS (WIP → out)
Stock moves to WIP at delivery, COGS booked at invoice time. Used for progress / milestone billing.
Aspect Sales Flow Project Flow
Delivery document Delivery Note (standard) Delivery Note WIP
Billing model Per delivery Progress / milestone based
Stock movement on delivery Stock goes out of warehouse (SLE created by DN) Stock transfers to WIP warehouse (SLE created by auto Material Transfer)
COGS entry At Delivery Note (Dr COGS / Cr Stock) At Material Issue after invoice (Dr COGS / Cr Stock WIP)
Revenue / Tax entry At Sales Invoice At Sales Invoice
Warehouse Finished Goods / Source warehouse Work In Progress (WIP) warehouse

4. Delivery Note WIP (Project Flow) — What Happens on Submit

What it is

Delivery Note WIP is used to record deliveries that are still being processed — for example, goods handed over to the site team but not yet finalized for invoicing.

What the DN WIP document itself does

  • Records the items, quantity, and warehouse being delivered.
  • Updates the linked Sales Order / Project Budget to show how much quantity has been delivered.
  • Tracks the billing status (how much has been invoiced).

What the DN WIP document itself does NOT do

  • It does not create any Stock Ledger Entry directly.
  • It does not create any accounting (GL) entry directly.
  • It does not reduce the stock balance in the books directly.
But — the DN WIP auto-creates a Stock Entry: When a DN WIP is submitted, the system automatically creates and submits a Stock Entry of type "Material Transfer" in the background. This Stock Entry moves the goods from the source warehouse to the target (WIP) warehouse. It is this Stock Entry — not the DN WIP itself — that creates the Stock Ledger Entries and GL entries.

What happens step by step when a DN WIP is submitted

1
Updates the Project Budget — marks the delivered quantity against each Project Budget item.
2
Updates the Sales Order — updates the per_delivered percentage.
3
Auto-creates a Material Transfer Stock Entry — moves stock from the source warehouse to the target (WIP) warehouse, valued at the item's rate.
4
Auto-submits the Stock Entry — the Stock Entry is submitted immediately, which creates the Stock Ledger Entries and (if perpetual inventory is enabled) the GL entries.

What happens when a DN WIP is cancelled

1
Cancels the auto-created Stock Entry — the system finds the Material Transfer Stock Entry linked to this DN WIP and cancels it, which reverses the stock movement and GL entries.
2
Reverts the Project Budget delivered quantities — reduces the delivered_qty back to what it was before the DN WIP was submitted.

5. Material Transfer (Auto-created from DN WIP) — Accounting Entries

What it is

When a DN WIP is submitted, the system automatically creates a Stock Entry with the type "Material Transfer". This Stock Entry moves the delivered items from the source warehouse (e.g. Finished Goods) to the target warehouse (Work In Progress — WIP).

Stock movement

Field Value
Stock Entry Type Material Transfer
Source Warehouse (s_warehouse) The warehouse from the DN WIP item (e.g. Finished Goods)
Target Warehouse (t_warehouse) The target warehouse from the DN WIP item (WIP warehouse)
Valuation Rate The item's rate from the DN WIP
Reference DN WIP name (reference_number), custom_reference_type = "Delivery Note WIP"

Accounting entry on submission (normal delivery)

The Material Transfer Stock Entry creates the following GL entry only if perpetual inventory is enabled for the company. The entry is valued at the stock (cost) value of the goods being transferred:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Stock / Inventory (target — WIP warehouse account) Stock value of items transferred
   Cr Stock / Inventory (source warehouse account) Stock value of items transferred
In simple words: Stock is debited in the WIP warehouse and credited from the source warehouse. The total stock value does not change — it just moves from one warehouse account to another.

Accounting entry on a DN WIP return

When the DN WIP is a return, the system auto-creates a Material Transfer Stock Entry with negative quantity, which reverses the stock movement:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Stock / Inventory (source warehouse account) Stock value of items returned
   Cr Stock / Inventory (target — WIP warehouse account) Stock value of items returned

What happens when the DN WIP is cancelled

The auto-created Material Transfer Stock Entry is cancelled, which reverses both the Stock Ledger Entries and the GL entries. Stock moves back from the WIP warehouse to the source warehouse.

Important: If the auto-created Stock Entry has already been invoiced (a Material Issue has been created from the Stock In Hand WIP report), cancelling the DN WIP may fail or leave inconsistencies. Always check that no Sales Invoice has been raised against the delivered items before cancelling a DN WIP.

6. Material Issue from Stock In Hand WIP — Accounting Entries

What it is

After a Sales Invoice is created and submitted for a Project-type order, the system automatically creates a Stock Entry of type "Material Issue" to move the delivered-and-now-invoiced items out of the WIP warehouse. This is the step where the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is finally booked in the Project flow.

This can be triggered in two ways:

  • Automatically — when a Project-type Sales Invoice is submitted (via the update_stock_on_si hook).
  • Manually — from the Stock In Hand WIP report, by clicking the "Process Stock Issue" button (via the process_stock_issue function).

Stock movement

Field Value
Stock Entry Type Material Issue
Source Warehouse (s_warehouse) Work In Progress (WIP) warehouse
Target Warehouse None — stock goes out (consumed)
Quantity Delivered qty − Billed qty (only the quantity that has been invoiced)
Valuation Rate Rate with overheads from the Project Budget
Reference Sales Invoice name (reference_number), custom_reference_type = "Sales Invoice"

Accounting entry on Material Issue (normal invoice)

The Material Issue Stock Entry creates the following GL entry only if perpetual inventory is enabled for the company. This is the COGS entry for the Project flow:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Cost of Goods Sold Stock value of items issued
   Cr Stock / Inventory (WIP warehouse account) Stock value of items issued
In simple words: Cost of Goods Sold is debited, and the WIP warehouse stock account is credited. This is the point where the cost of the goods is finally recognized in the Project flow — at invoice time, not at delivery time.

Accounting entry on a Sales Invoice return (Credit Note)

When a Project-type Sales Invoice return (Credit Note) is submitted, the system auto-creates a Material Receipt Stock Entry to move the returned items back into the WIP warehouse, reversing the COGS:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Stock / Inventory (WIP warehouse account) Stock value of items returned
   Cr Cost of Goods Sold Stock value of items returned

What happens when the Sales Invoice is cancelled

The auto-created Material Issue Stock Entry is cancelled, which reverses both the Stock Ledger Entries and the GL entries. Stock is added back to the WIP warehouse, and the COGS / Inventory entries are reversed. The billed quantities in the Project Budget are also reverted.

7. Delivery Note (Sales Flow) — Accounting Entries

What it is

The Delivery Note is the standard document used to issue goods out of the warehouse to a customer. It reduces the physical stock and — when the company's settings allow it — creates the related accounting entries.

What happens when a Delivery Note is submitted

1
Updates the Sales Order — marks the delivered quantity.
2
Updates the billing status — tracks how much has been invoiced.
3
Updates the Stock Ledger — records the outgoing stock movement for each item. This reduces the stock quantity in the warehouse.
4
Creates Accounting Entries (GL) — but only if the company is set up for perpetual inventory (see section 8).

Accounting entry created on a normal Delivery Note

For each item delivered, the system posts the following entry, valued at the stock (cost) value of the goods — not at the selling price:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Cost of Goods Sold Stock value of items delivered
   Cr Stock / Inventory (warehouse account) Stock value of items delivered
In simple words: Cost of Goods Sold is debited, and the Stock (Inventory) account of the warehouse is credited — for the cost value of the goods going out.

Accounting entry on a Delivery Note Return

When the Delivery Note is a return (goods coming back from the customer), the same entry is reversed:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Stock / Inventory (warehouse account) Stock value of items returned
   Cr Cost of Goods Sold Stock value of items returned

What happens when a Delivery Note is cancelled

When a Delivery Note is cancelled, the system reverses the original stock movement and the original accounting entries. The stock is added back to the warehouse, and the Cost of Goods Sold / Inventory entries are reversed.

8. When Does the Delivery Note Create Accounting Entries?

The Delivery Note (and the auto-created Stock Entries from DN WIP / Sales Invoice) create accounting entries only when at least one of the following is enabled for the company:

Setting Where it is set
Perpetual Inventory enabled Company settings
Provisional accounting for non-stock items enabled Company settings
The document contains a fixed asset item Item master

If none of these are enabled, the Delivery Note / Stock Entry will:

  • Still create the Stock Ledger Entry (stock quantity will move), but
  • Not create any accounting (GL) entry.
Troubleshooting: This is the most common reason a Delivery Note or Stock Entry shows a stock movement but no accounting voucher. If accounting entries are expected but not appearing, please check with the system administrator whether Perpetual Inventory is enabled for the company.

9. Where is the Revenue / Customer Entry Booked?

Neither the Delivery Note, the DN WIP, nor the auto-created Stock Entries book the sale revenue, the customer (debtor), or the taxes.

Those are booked when the Sales Invoice is created — either:

  • Directly, or
  • From the Delivery Note (using the "Create → Sales Invoice" button), or
  • From the Delivery Note WIP (using the "Create → Sales Invoice" button).

The Sales Invoice creates entries of the form:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Customer (Debtor) Invoice value (incl. tax)
   Cr Sales Income Net value
   Cr Tax Payable (VAT / etc.) Tax amount
Note: In the Project flow, submitting the Sales Invoice also triggers the auto-creation of the Material Issue Stock Entry (see section 6), which books the COGS entry. So in the Project flow, both revenue and COGS are booked at invoice time.

Sales Flow — Returns

Action What happens Accounting impact
DN Return submitted Stock moves back into warehouse (SLE reversed) Dr Stock / Cr COGS (reversal)
DN cancelled Stock moves back, SLE & GL reversed All entries reversed

Project Flow — Returns

Action What happens Accounting impact
DN WIP Return submitted Auto-creates Material Transfer SE with negative qty — stock moves back from WIP to source Dr Stock (source) / Cr Stock (WIP) (reversal)
DN WIP cancelled Auto-created Material Transfer SE is cancelled — stock & GL reversed All entries reversed
Sales Invoice return (Credit Note) submitted Auto-creates Material Receipt SE — stock moves back into WIP warehouse Dr Stock (WIP) / Cr COGS (reversal) + revenue/tax reversal

Sales Flow

Project Flow

12. Important Reminders

  • DN WIP is a non-posting document, but it auto-creates a Material Transfer Stock Entry. The DN WIP itself does not create SLE or GL entries. However, submitting a DN WIP triggers the system to create and submit a Material Transfer Stock Entry, which moves stock from the source warehouse to the WIP warehouse and creates SLE and GL entries (if perpetual inventory is enabled).
  • Delivery Note is a posting document. On submission it always reduces stock, and it creates accounting entries only if perpetual inventory (or provisional accounting, or an asset item) is enabled for the company.
  • Material Transfer (from DN WIP): Moves stock from source warehouse to WIP warehouse. GL entry: Dr Stock (WIP) / Cr Stock (source). No COGS is booked at this stage — the stock just changes location.
  • Material Issue (from Stock In Hand WIP / Sales Invoice): Moves stock out of the WIP warehouse. GL entry: Dr COGS / Cr Stock (WIP). This is where the cost of goods sold is recognized in the Project flow.
  • Material Receipt (from Credit Note return): Moves stock back into the WIP warehouse. GL entry: Dr Stock (WIP) / Cr COGS (reversal).
  • The accounting entry on a Delivery Note is a stock-vs-cost entry, valued at the cost of the goods — not the selling price:
    • Dr Cost of Goods Sold / Cr Stock Inventory for a normal delivery.
    • Reversed for a return.
    • Reversed again when the Delivery Note is cancelled.
  • Revenue, customer, and tax entries are always booked by the Sales Invoice, never by the Delivery Note, DN WIP, or Stock Entries.
  • In the Project flow, COGS is booked at invoice time — via the Material Issue Stock Entry auto-created from the Sales Invoice or triggered from the Stock In Hand WIP report, not at delivery time.
  • Cancelling a DN WIP cancels the auto-created Material Transfer Stock Entry, which reverses the stock and GL entries. Always ensure no Sales Invoice has been raised before cancelling.
  • Cancelling a Sales Invoice cancels the auto-created Material Issue Stock Entry, which reverses the COGS and stock entries. The billed quantities in the Project Budget are also reverted.
  • If a document shows stock movement but no accounting entry, check whether Perpetual Inventory is enabled for the company.
Tip: Always create the Delivery Note / Delivery Note WIP from the Sales Order to ensure the delivered quantity is tracked correctly and the billing flow works properly. In the Project flow, always run the Stock In Hand WIP report after invoicing to ensure the Material Issue Stock Entries are created and COGS is booked.
