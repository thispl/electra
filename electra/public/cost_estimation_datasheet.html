1. System Overview & Document Hierarchy

The Cost Estimation module operates through a two-level document hierarchy:

Document Level Description
Cost Estimation Parent The main document that aggregates all Scope of Work (SoW) lines, computes overall totals, and links to Quotation / Sales Order.
CE SOW (Cost Estimation — Scope of Work) Child One per Master Scope of Work (MSOW) row. Contains all line items (Design, Materials, Installation, Manpower, etc.) and performs the actual cost & profit calculations.

The typical flow is:

Cost Estimation (Parent) → CE SOW (Child, per MSOW) → Line Items (Design, Materials, Finishing, Accessories, Installation, Manpower, Heavy Equipments, Subcontract, Finished Goods, Raw Materials)

Calculations happen bottom-up: line items → section sub-totals → CE SOW totals → Cost Estimation roll-up.

2. Line Item Level Calculations

Each row within a section (Design, Materials, Installation, etc.) has the following computed fields:

Field Formula Description
Amount (Cost Amount) Amount = Qty × Unit Price The base estimated cost for that line item at the cost/valuation rate.
Cost Amount Cost Amount = Qty × Cost Rate Cost rate is fetched from the last valuation rate of the item in the selected company.
Amount with Overheads (Selling Amount) Amount with Overheads = Qty × Rate with Overheads The selling price amount after overhead & profit distribution (see Section 3).
Rate with Overheads (Selling Rate) Rate with Overheads = (Amount + Proportionate Overhead) / Qty Selling price per unit after overhead apportionment (see Section 3).

Manpower Line Items (Special Case)

For manpower rows, the calculation uses worker count, working hours, and days:

Rate = Unit Price × Working Hours × Days
Amount = Total Workers × Rate
Cost Amount = Total Workers × Cost Rate × Working Hours × Days

3. Section-Level Totals (Cost, Selling Price, Profit)

Each section (Design, Materials, Raw Materials, Finishing Work, Accessories, Installation, Manpower, Heavy Equipments, Subcontract, Finished Goods) computes three sub-totals:

Field Formula Description
Section Amount
(e.g., materials_amount)		 Σ(line item Amount) Sum of all Amount values (Qty × Unit Price) in that section.
Section Amount with Overheads
(e.g., materials_amount_with_overheads)		 Σ(line item Amount with Overheads) Sum of all selling price amounts in that section.
Section Profit
(e.g., materials_profit)		 Section Amount with Overheads − Section Amount The profit earned in that specific section.
Section Profit % (Section Profit / Section Amount with Overheads) × 100 Profit percentage for that section (shown in the HTML view on each CE SOW).
Note: The same pattern applies to all 10 sections: Design, Raw Materials, Supply Materials, Finishing Work, Accessories, Installation, Manpower, Heavy Equipments, Subcontract (Others), and Finished Goods.

4. Selling Price Distribution (Overhead Apportionment)

The system distributes total overheads and net profit proportionally across all line items based on each item's share of the total estimated cost.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Total SPA (Selling Price Amount) = Σ(all line item Amounts across all sections)

Step 2: SP Distribution % = (Item Amount / Total SPA)

Step 3: Total Additions = Total Overheads + Net Profit Amount

Step 4: Proportionate Overhead = SP Distribution % × Total Additions

Step 5: SPA with Overhead = Item Amount + Proportionate Overhead

Step 6: Rate with Overheads (Selling Rate) = SPA with Overhead / Qty

Step 7: Amount with Overheads (Selling Amount) = Qty × Rate with Overheads
Key Principle: Items with higher cost amounts receive a proportionally higher share of overheads and profit. This ensures the selling price of each item reflects its true share of the total project cost structure.

5. Total Cost of the Project

The total cost is the sum of all section-level estimated cost amounts:

Total Cost = Design Amount
  + Raw Materials Amount
  + Materials Amount
  + Finishing Work Amount
  + Accessories Amount
  + Installation Amount
  + Manpower Amount
  + Heavy Equipments Amount
  + Subcontract (Others) Amount
  + Finished Goods Amount

This is the base cost before any overheads, profit, or business promotion are added.

6. Overhead Calculations

Three types of overheads are calculated as percentages of the Total Cost:

Field Formula Description
Overhead Amount
(total_amount_as_overhead)		 Total Cost × (Overhead % / 100) General overheads (admin, facilities, etc.) as a percentage of total cost.
Engineering Overhead Amount
(total_amount_as_engineering_overhead)		 Total Cost × (Engineering Overhead % / 100) Engineering-specific overheads as a percentage of total cost.
Contingency Amount
(contigency)		 Total Cost × (Contingency % / 100) Contingency buffer as a percentage of total cost.
Total Overheads
(total_overheads)		 Overhead Amount + Engineering Overhead Amount + Contingency Amount The combined overhead to be added to the total cost.
Reverse Calculation: If the amount is entered instead of the percentage, the system reverse-calculates: Overhead % = (Overhead Amount / Total Cost) × 100

7. Gross Profit All Divisions

Gross Profit represents the total profit from all sections before deducting overheads and discounts.

Gross Profit Amount

Gross Profit Amount = Design Profit
  + Raw Materials Profit
  + Materials Profit
  + Finishing Work Profit
  + Accessories Profit
  + Installation Profit
  + Manpower Profit
  + Heavy Equipments Profit
  + Subcontract (Others) Profit
  + Finished Goods Profit
  − Discount Tolerance Amount

Where each section's profit = Section Amount with Overheads − Section Amount

Gross Profit %

Gross Profit % = (Gross Profit Amount / Total Bidding Price) × 100

8. Net Profit All Divisions

Net Profit is the user-defined profit margin applied on top of the total cost. It can be entered as either an amount or a percentage — the system auto-calculates the other.

If entering… Formula Result
Net Profit Amount Net Profit % = (Net Profit Amount / Total Cost) × 100 Percentage is auto-calculated
Net Profit % Net Profit Amount = Total Cost × (Net Profit % / 100) Amount is auto-calculated

Net Profit after Discount (displayed in Total Cost popup)

Net Profit Amount (after discount) = Gross Profit Amount − Discount Tolerance Amount
Net Profit % (after discount) = Gross Profit % − Discount Tolerance %

Net Profit % (Non-MEP, from Total Cost popup)

Net Profit % = (Net Profit Amount / (Total Cost + Gross Profit Amount)) × 100

9. Discount Tolerance

Discount Tolerance represents the maximum discount that can be offered to the client while maintaining profitability.

Field Formula Description
Discount Tolerance Amount Total Bidding Price × (Discount Tolerance % / 100) The monetary value of the discount.
Discount Tolerance % (Discount Tolerance Amount / Total Bidding Price) × 100 Reverse calculation if amount is entered.
Discount Upto Total Bidding Price − Discount Tolerance Amount The minimum price after discount is applied.
Note: At the Cost Estimation (parent) level, SoW-level discount tolerances are aggregated: SoW Discount Tolerance % and SoW Discount Tolerance Amount are the sums of all MSOW rows' discount values.

10. Business Promotion

Business Promotion is an additional amount for marketing/business development activities, calculated as a percentage of total cost.

If entering… Formula Result
Business Promotion Amount Business Promotion % = (Business Promotion Amount / Total Cost) × 100 Percentage is auto-calculated
Business Promotion % Business Promotion Amount = Total Cost × (Business Promotion % / 100) Amount is auto-calculated

11. Total Bidding Price

The Total Bidding Price is the final quoted price submitted to the client. The calculation differs by division:

Non-MEP Divisions General

Total Bidding Price = Total Cost
  + Total Overheads (Overhead + Engineering Overhead + Contingency)
  + Net Profit Amount
  + Discount Tolerance Amount

MEP Division MEP Only

Total Bidding Price = Total Cost
  + MEP Net Profit Amount
  + Discount Tolerance Amount

Where for MEP:

MEP Net Profit Amount = Gross Profit Amount − (Overhead Amount + Contingency Amount + Engineering Overhead Amount)

MEP Net Profit % = (MEP Net Profit Amount / Total Bidding Price) × 100

Total Cost Popup Display (Both Divisions)

When the "Total Cost" button is clicked on a submitted Cost Estimation, the system displays a summary table with the following breakdown:

Total Bidding Price = Total Cost of the Project
  + Net Profit (after discount) [= Gross Profit Amount − Discount Tolerance Amount]
  + Total Overheads [= Overhead + Engineering Overhead + Contingency]
  + Total Business Promotion

12. Unit Price (Per MSOW)

The unit price for each Master Scope of Work line is derived from the total bidding price:

Unit Price = Total Bidding Price / Qty (of the MSOW)

13. MEP Division — Special Calculations MEP Only

The MEP Division uses a different cost model for materials. MEP materials involve transfer charges between companies.

MEP Material Line Item Calculations

Field Formula Description
Transfer Charges Valuation Rate × (Transfer % / 100) Transfer charge per unit based on the item group's transfer percentage.
Transfer Amount Qty × Transfer Charges Total transfer charges for the line item.
Cost (with Transfer) Valuation Rate × (1 + Transfer % / 100) Cost per unit including transfer charges.
Transfer Cost Amount Qty × Cost (with Transfer) Total cost amount including transfer charges.
Selling Amount Qty × Unit Price Selling amount (Unit Price is the selling price).
Profit Amount Selling Amount − Transfer Cost Amount Profit for the MEP material line.
Profit % (Profit Amount / Selling Amount) × 100 Profit percentage for the MEP material line.
MEP Key Difference: In MEP, the "materials_amount" field is set to the Transfer Cost Amount (not the raw cost), and "materials_amount_with_overheads" is set to the Selling Amount. Total Overheads is set to 0 for MEP — overheads are embedded within the Gross Profit calculation instead.

14. Cost Estimation (Parent) — Roll-up Aggregation

When the Cost Estimation document is validated/saved, it aggregates values from all non-optional MSOW rows in the Master Scope of Work table:

Parent Field Formula Description
Total Cost of the Project Σ(MSOW Total Cost) Sum of total cost across all non-optional MSOW rows.
Total Bidding Price Σ(MSOW Total Bidding Price) Sum of bidding prices across all non-optional MSOW rows.
Total Amount as Overhead Σ(MSOW Overhead Amount) Aggregated overhead amount.
Total Amount as Engineering Overhead Σ(MSOW Engineering Overhead Amount) Aggregated engineering overhead amount.
Contingency Σ(MSOW Contingency Amount) Aggregated contingency amount.
Overhead % (Total Amount as Overhead / Total Cost) × 100 Overall overhead percentage.
Engineering Overhead % (Total Amount as Engineering Overhead / Total Cost) × 100 Overall engineering overhead percentage.
Contingency % (Contingency / Total Cost) × 100 Overall contingency percentage.
Gross Profit Amount Σ(MSOW Gross Profit Amount) Aggregated gross profit.
Gross Profit % Σ(MSOW Gross Profit %) Aggregated gross profit percentage.
Net Profit Amount Σ(MSOW Net Profit Amount) Aggregated net profit amount.
Net Profit % Σ(MSOW Net Profit %) Aggregated net profit percentage.
Total Business Promotion Σ(MSOW Business Promotion Amount) Aggregated business promotion.
SoW Discount Tolerance % Σ(MSOW Discount Tolerance %) Aggregated discount tolerance percentage.
SoW Discount Tolerance Amount Σ(MSOW Discount Tolerance Amount) Aggregated discount tolerance amount.
Note: Only MSOW rows where Optional = 0 (not marked as optional) are included in the aggregation.
