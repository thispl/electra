1. What is Bank Reconciliation?

A Bank Reconciliation entry is used to match ERPNext account statements with your bank account statements. At the end of a financial period, you may have mailed a cheque to a Supplier that has not yet been cleared by the bank. Or, a customer may have deposited money into your account, which is not yet reflected in your ERPNext system.

In ERPNext, you can synchronise your bank statements and your Journal Entries using the transaction dates. The Bank Reconciliation Report provides the difference between the bank balance shown in your bank statement (as provided by the bank) and the amount shown in your company's Chart of Accounts.

In the report, check whether the field "Balance as per bank" matches the Bank Account Statement. If it matches, the Clearance Date is correctly updated for all bank entries. If there is a mismatch, it means some bank entries do not yet have a Clearance Date updated.

2. Types of Reconciliation Tools in ERPNext

ERPNext provides two reconciliation tools:

Tool Description
Manual Reconciliation Allows you to set clearance dates against payment entries, sales invoice payments, or journal entries.
Semi-Automatic Reconciliation Allows you to clear bank transactions against payment entries, sales and purchase invoice payments, journal entries, or expense claims.

3. Manual Bank Reconciliation (Bank Clearance)

The manual reconciliation tool allows you to update clearance dates on existing payment entries and journal entries to match them with your bank statement.

To access the manual reconciliation tool, go to:

Home > Accounting > Bank Statement > Bank Clearance

1
Go to Bank Clearance.
2
Select your Bank Account.
3
Select a From Date and To Date.
4
You can choose to include reconciled entries and POS transactions.
5
Click on the "Get Payment Entries" button. All "Bank Voucher" type entries will be displayed.
6
In each of the entries, on the rightmost column, update the "Clearance Date" field.
7
Click on the "Update Clearance Date" button to save. This syncs your bank statements with the entries in the system.

4. Bank Reconciliation Statement Report

To view the Bank Reconciliation Statement report, go to:

Accounts > Bank Statement > Bank Reconciliation Statement

In the report, check whether the field "Balance as per bank" matches the Bank Account Statement:

  • If it matches, the Clearance Date is correctly updated for all bank entries.
  • If there is a mismatch, some bank entries do not yet have a Clearance Date updated.
Tip: Use this report at the end of each month to verify that your ERPNext bank balance matches your actual bank statement balance.

5. Semi-Automatic Bank Reconciliation Tool

The semi-automatic reconciliation tool is a two-step process:

1
Add Bank Transactions into ERPNext via Bank Statement Import or Bank Account Synchronization.
2
Reconcile the Bank Statement using the Bank Reconciliation Tool.

To access the Bank Reconciliation Tool, go to:

Accounting > Bank Statement > Bank Reconciliation Tool

Or simply search for "Bank Reconciliation Tool" in the awesome bar.

6. Importing Bank Statements

You can upload a Bank Statement in CSV or XLSX format into ERPNext using the Bank Statement Import tool.

1
Go to Accounting > Bank Statement > Bank Statement Import or search for "Bank Statement Import" in the awesome bar.
2
Select your Bank Account.
3
Upload your bank statement file (CSV or XLSX).
4
ERPNext will read the file and map the columns to the corresponding fields in the Bank Transaction DocType.
5
The mapping is stored in the Bank document linked to the corresponding Bank Account. On the next upload, the mapping is taken from here, but you can change it if needed.
6
The Bank Transactions will be imported via a background job. The progress will be shown on the screen.
Note: Ensure your bank accounts are properly configured in ERPNext with the correct account numbers and GL links before importing transactions.

7. Reconciling Bank Transactions

Once all your bank transactions are imported into ERPNext, you can reconcile them with your existing vouchers using the Bank Reconciliation Tool.

1
Go to Accounting > Bank Statement > Bank Reconciliation Tool or search for "Bank Reconciliation Tool" in the awesome bar.
2
Select your Company, Bank Account, Bank Statement Start Date, and End Date.
3
Make sure that the opening balance from ERPNext matches the opening balance of your Bank Statement.
4
Enter the Closing Balance of the Bank Statement.
5
Saving the document will show the matching bank transactions.
6
The final goal of Bank Reconciliation is to make the difference amount zero (green) by either matching to an existing voucher or creating a new voucher.
7
For all bank transactions that do not have a clearance date, click on the Actions Button to Match or Create Vouchers.

8. Creating New Vouchers from Bank Transactions

If a bank transaction does not match any existing voucher, you can create a new voucher directly from the Bank Reconciliation Tool:

1
Select the bank transaction and click on the Actions button.
2
Choose "Create Voucher" in the Action dropdown.
3
Select the document type (Payment Entry, Journal Entry, etc.).
4
Fill in the details that were not available in the Bank Transaction.
5
Click on "Submit" to create the corresponding voucher and update its clearance date.

9. Matching Bank Transactions to Existing Vouchers

To match a bank transaction against an existing voucher in the system:

1
Select the bank transaction and click on the Actions button.
2
Choose "Match Against Voucher" in the Action dropdown.
3
The vouchers related to this transaction will be displayed. They will be ranked based on the maximum number of fields matched.
4
You can match one or multiple vouchers against the same Bank Transaction using the checkboxes.
5
Click "Reconcile" to confirm the match. The clearance date will be updated automatically.

10. Updating Bank Transactions

Updating a Bank Transaction can help ERPNext find better matches during reconciliation:

1
Select the bank transaction and click on the Actions button.
2
Choose "Update Bank Transaction" in the Action dropdown.
3
Fill in the required details (e.g., party, reference number, description).
4
Click on "Submit" to save the updated Bank Transaction.
Tip: Adding party information and reference numbers to bank transactions significantly improves the quality of automatic match suggestions.

11. Bank Transaction Doctype

The Bank Transaction form shows bank transactions imported into ERPNext, typically from bank statements or integrations. A Bank Transaction is typically not created manually. It can be imported or created using:

  • Bank Statement Import — import from CSV or XLSX files
  • Data Import — standard ERPNext data import tool
  • Bank integration apps — e.g., Plaid or other third-party integrations

Once imported, Bank Transactions can be reconciled with vouchers using the Bank Reconciliation Tool. The Payment Entries table within a Bank Transaction links it to vouchers in ERPNext for reconciliation.

Each Bank Transaction shows:

  • Date: The transaction date as per the bank statement
  • Description: The narrative/description from the bank statement
  • Type: Whether it is a Debit (withdrawal) or Credit (deposit)
  • Amount: The transaction amount
  • Status: Whether the transaction is unreconciled or reconciled
  • Party: The matched customer or supplier (if identified)

12. Important Reminders

  • Always verify that the Bank Account is correctly linked to the appropriate GL account in ERPNext before starting reconciliation.
  • Check that the opening balance in ERPNext matches the opening balance on your bank statement before reconciling.
  • The goal of bank reconciliation is to make the difference amount zero. If the difference is not zero, some transactions are missing or incorrectly matched.
  • After reconciliation, always check the Bank Reconciliation Statement report to confirm the "Balance as per bank" matches your bank statement.
  • Use the Update Bank Transaction action to add party and reference information to improve automatic matching.
  • For the manual reconciliation tool, always update the Clearance Date on payment entries to match the date the transaction cleared the bank.
  • Regularly import bank statements to keep the unreconciled transaction list up to date.
  • Bank transactions that are not matched to any voucher will remain unreconciled and will cause a difference in the Bank Reconciliation Statement.
