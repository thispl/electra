1. Purpose

This document explains, in simple terms, how accounting (ledger) entries are created when goods are delivered to a customer using a Delivery Note in the Electra ERP system.

It covers the two delivery documents used in the system:

  • Delivery Note — the main posting document used to issue goods to a customer (Sales flow).
  • Delivery Note WIP — a working / tracking document used to record deliveries that are still in progress (Project flow).

The document explains what each one does, when it creates accounting entries, and what those entries look like.

2. Posting vs Non-posting Documents

Type Meaning Creates Accounting Entries?
Posting document A document that affects the books of accounts (General Ledger) and stock registers. Yes
Non-posting document A document used only to record or track activity. It does not affect the books. No

In the Electra system:

  • Delivery Note is a posting document.
  • Delivery Note WIP is a non-posting document.
Key point: This is the single most important distinction to remember. The Delivery Note creates stock and accounting entries on submission. The Delivery Note WIP does not.

3. Two Business Flows in Electra ERP

Electra ERP handles two distinct types of sales transactions, each with a different document flow and different timing for stock movement and accounting entries:

Sales Flow
Sales Order → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice
  ↑ stock out + COGS
         ↑ revenue booked
Stock and COGS booked at delivery time. Used for regular trading sales.
Project Flow
Sales Order → DN WIP → Sales Invoice → Material Issue
  ↑ no stock / no GL
       ↑ revenue
                 ↑ stock out + COGS
Stock and COGS booked at invoice time. Used for progress / milestone billing.
Aspect Sales Flow Project Flow
Delivery document Delivery Note (standard) Delivery Note WIP
Billing model Per delivery Progress / milestone based
Stock goes out when At Delivery Note (immediately) At Material Issue (after invoice)
COGS / Inventory entry At Delivery Note At Material Issue (after invoice)
Revenue / Tax entry At Sales Invoice At Sales Invoice
Warehouse Finished Goods / Source warehouse Work In Progress (WIP) warehouse

4. Delivery Note WIP (Project Flow)

What it is

Delivery Note WIP is used to record deliveries that are still being processed — for example, goods handed over to the site team but not yet finalized for invoicing.

What it does

  • Records the items, quantity, and warehouse being delivered.
  • Updates the linked Sales Order to show how much quantity has been delivered.
  • Tracks the billing status (how much has been invoiced).

What it does NOT do

  • It does not create any Stock Ledger Entry.
  • It does not create any accounting (GL) entry.
  • It does not reduce the stock balance in the books.
Important: Submitting a Delivery Note WIP produces zero stock movement and zero accounting entries. Stock stays in the WIP warehouse. This is by design — for the Project flow, everything happens at invoice time.

When does accounting happen for a WIP delivery?

Accounting happens through two downstream documents:

1
Sales Invoice (created from the DN WIP) — books the revenue, customer (debtor), and taxes.
2
Material Issue (Stock Entry) (triggered from the Stock In Hand WIP report) — moves stock out of the WIP warehouse and books the COGS / Inventory entry.

Return through Delivery Note WIP

When a return is created using Delivery Note WIP with the "Issue Credit Note" option ticked, the system automatically creates a Credit Note (Sales Invoice) for the returned goods. The accounting for the return is then handled by that Credit Note — not by the Delivery Note WIP itself.

5. Delivery Note (Sales Flow)

What it is

The Delivery Note is the standard document used to issue goods out of the warehouse to a customer. It reduces the physical stock and — when the company's settings allow it — creates the related accounting entries.

What happens when a Delivery Note is submitted

1
Updates the Sales Order — marks the delivered quantity.
2
Updates the billing status — tracks how much has been invoiced.
3
Updates the Stock Ledger — records the outgoing stock movement for each item. This reduces the stock quantity in the warehouse.
4
Creates Accounting Entries (GL) — but only if the company is set up for perpetual inventory (see section 6).

Accounting entry created on a normal Delivery Note

For each item delivered, the system posts the following entry, valued at the stock (cost) value of the goods — not at the selling price:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Cost of Goods Sold Stock value of items delivered
   Cr Stock / Inventory (warehouse account) Stock value of items delivered
In simple words: Cost of Goods Sold is debited, and the Stock (Inventory) account of the warehouse is credited — for the cost value of the goods going out.

Accounting entry on a Delivery Note Return

When the Delivery Note is a return (goods coming back from the customer), the same entry is reversed:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Stock / Inventory (warehouse account) Stock value of items returned
   Cr Cost of Goods Sold Stock value of items returned

What happens when a Delivery Note is cancelled

When a Delivery Note is cancelled, the system reverses the original stock movement and the original accounting entries. The stock is added back to the warehouse, and the Cost of Goods Sold / Inventory entries are reversed.

6. When Does the Delivery Note Create Accounting Entries?

The Delivery Note creates accounting entries only when at least one of the following is enabled for the company:

Setting Where it is set
Perpetual Inventory enabled Company settings
Provisional accounting for non-stock items enabled Company settings
The document contains a fixed asset item Item master

If none of these are enabled, the Delivery Note will:

  • Still create the Stock Ledger Entry (stock quantity will reduce), but
  • Not create any accounting (GL) entry.
Troubleshooting: This is the most common reason a Delivery Note shows a stock movement but no accounting voucher. If accounting entries are expected but not appearing, please check with the system administrator whether Perpetual Inventory is enabled for the company.

7. Where is the Revenue / Customer Entry Booked?

A Delivery Note books only the stock-vs-cost movement. It does not book the sale revenue, the customer (debtor), or the taxes.

Those are booked when the Sales Invoice is created — either:

  • Directly, or
  • From the Delivery Note (using the "Create → Sales Invoice" button), or
  • From the Delivery Note WIP (using the "Create → Sales Invoice" button).

The Sales Invoice creates entries of the form:

Account Debit Credit
Dr Customer (Debtor) Invoice value (incl. tax)
   Cr Sales Income Net value
   Cr Tax Payable (VAT / etc.) Tax amount
Note: If the Sales Invoice is created with the "Update Stock" option enabled, it will also create the same Cost of Goods Sold / Inventory entry as a Delivery Note.

8. Complete Flow — From Sales Order to Accounting

Step Document Stock Movement Accounting Entry
1 Sales Order None None (Sales Order is not a posting document)
2a Delivery Note (Sales flow) Yes — stock goes out Yes — Dr COGS / Cr Stock Inventory (only if perpetual inventory enabled)
2b Delivery Note WIP (Project flow) No None — only updates the Sales Order delivery status
3 Sales Invoice Only if "Update Stock" is enabled Yes — Dr Customer / Cr Sales Income + Cr Tax Payable (+ COGS/Stock if Update Stock enabled)
4 Material Issue (Project flow only) Yes — stock out of WIP warehouse Yes — Dr COGS / Cr Stock (WIP) (only if perpetual inventory enabled)

9. Important Reminders

  • Delivery Note WIP is a tracking document. It does not create any stock or accounting entries. It only updates the Sales Order delivery status. The actual accounting is done later by the Sales Invoice and Material Issue.
  • Delivery Note is the posting document. On submission it always reduces stock, and it creates accounting entries only if perpetual inventory (or provisional accounting, or an asset item) is enabled for the company.
  • The accounting entry on a Delivery Note is a stock-vs-cost entry, valued at the cost of the goods — not the selling price:
    • Dr Cost of Goods Sold / Cr Stock Inventory for a normal delivery.
    • Reversed for a return.
    • Reversed again when the Delivery Note is cancelled.
  • Revenue, customer, and tax entries are always booked by the Sales Invoice, never by the Delivery Note.
  • In the Project flow, stock goes out at invoice time — via the Material Issue Stock Entry triggered from the Stock In Hand WIP report, not at delivery time.
  • If a Delivery Note shows stock movement but no accounting entry, check whether Perpetual Inventory is enabled for the company.
  • Returns in the Project flow follow the same pattern in reverse: the return DN WIP records the return without accounting impact; the Credit Note (auto-created) books the revenue/tax/customer reversal; the Material Receipt Stock Entry adds the stock back to the WIP warehouse.
Tip: Always create the Delivery Note / Delivery Note WIP from the Sales Order to ensure the delivered quantity is tracked correctly and the billing flow works properly.
