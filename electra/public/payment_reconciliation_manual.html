1. Overview

Payment Reconciliation is the process of linking payments with their corresponding invoices. In many business scenarios, especially when customers send block payments or payments on a schedule that is not directly linked to individual invoices, there is no automatic link between payments and invoices.

In such cases, you can match Payments with Invoices using the Payment Reconciliation tool in ERPNext. This ensures that:

  • Outstanding invoice amounts are accurately tracked
  • Advance payments are properly allocated against invoices
  • Ageing reports reflect the correct outstanding balances
  • Financial analysis and audits are accurate

Payment Reconciliation is a separate process from Bank Reconciliation. While Bank Reconciliation matches ERP entries with bank statement transactions, Payment Reconciliation matches unallocated payments (advances, credit notes) with open invoices for a given party.

2. Accessing Payment Reconciliation

1
Log into your ERPNext account.
2
Go to Accounting > Accounts Receivable > Payment Reconciliation (for customers) or Accounting > Accounts Payable > Payment Reconciliation (for suppliers).
Tip: You can also search for "Payment Reconciliation" directly in the awesome bar (search bar) at the top of the screen.

3. Selecting Filters & Getting Entries

Once you are on the Payment Reconciliation page, you need to set the following filters to fetch the unreconciled entries:

Field Description
Company Select the company for which you want to perform reconciliation.
Party Type Select either Customer (for Accounts Receivable) or Supplier (for Accounts Payable).
Party Select the specific customer or supplier. The Receivable/Payable account will be selected automatically.
Receivable / Payable Account This is auto-selected based on the party. Ensure it matches the account on the invoices.
Bank / Cash Account Select the bank or cash account against which the payments need to be reconciled.
Date Range Optionally, select a date range for the invoices and payment transactions to narrow down the results.
Minimum / Maximum Amount Optionally, set minimum or maximum amount filters for invoices and payment transactions.
1
After setting the filters, click on the "Get Unreconciled Entries" button.
2
ERPNext will fetch all un-linked invoices and payment transactions from that party. Invoices will appear in the Invoices table and payments in the Payments table.

4. Allocating Payments to Invoices

Once the unreconciled entries are fetched, you need to allocate payments against invoices:

1
You can either select specific entries to be allocated, or click the "Allocate" button without selecting anything to allocate all entries automatically.
2
The allocation table will be populated based on FIFO (First In First Out) method or based on your manual selection.
3
The Allocated Amount is the amount you want to allocate for the reconciliation. You can adjust this if partial allocation is needed.
Note: Partial allocation is supported. A single payment can be split across multiple invoices, and multiple payments can be applied to one invoice.

5. Reconciling the Entries

1
After reviewing the allocations, click on the "Reconcile" button to reconcile the allocated entries.
2
You will get a confirmation message that says "Successfully Reconciled".
Tip: After reconciliation, verify the results by checking the invoice status — the outstanding amount should be updated, and the invoice should be marked as "Paid" if the full amount has been allocated.

6. What Happens on Payment Reconciliation

  • Payment Entry vs. Invoice: Reconciliation against a Payment Entry does not automatically create a journal entry, because payment entries are already connected to transactions and the general ledger already reflects the effect.
  • Credit/Debit Note vs. Invoice: When reconciling an invoice against a credit or debit note, a journal entry is generated. This is required because these notes lack direct transaction links.
  • Invoice Outstanding: The outstanding amount on the invoice is updated. If the full amount is covered, the invoice is marked as "Paid". If partially allocated, the invoice shows the remaining due amount.
  • Ageing Reports: After reconciliation, ageing reports will accurately reflect the outstanding balances for the party.

7. Important Reminders

  • Always verify that the Receivable/Payable Account matches the account on the invoice. If accounts don't match, entries will not appear in the reconciliation.
  • Run Payment Reconciliation before chasing any customer balance — the outstanding may already be covered by an unallocated advance.
  • For high-volume environments where payments arrive in batches or as advances, the matching step is deferred. Make it a regular month-end activity to avoid backlogs.
  • After reconciliation, always check the General Ledger and Ageing Report to confirm the outstanding balances are correct.
  • If a payment was created directly against an invoice at the time of recording, ERPNext reconciles automatically on submit — no manual reconciliation is needed in that case.
